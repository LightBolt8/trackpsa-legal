Support

Version 1.0 · Updated May 16, 2026

TrackPSA helps you log PSA lab results and view trends. Need help? Email trackpsateam@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version when you can.

FAQ

  • Sign in: Apple or email — use the same method each time.
  • Backup: Readings sync when you add them (Settings → Cloud backup).
  • Sign out: Hides data on this device; sign in again to restore.
  • Delete one reading: History (swipe) or Home (trash icon).
  • Delete all: Settings → Clear all data.
  • Language: Settings → English or Chinese.
  • Medical disclaimer: Personal records only — not medical advice. Talk to your doctor about PSA results.
