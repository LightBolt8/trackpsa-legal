TrackPSA Privacy Policy

Effective May 16, 2026 · v1.0

What we collect

  • Account info (Firebase Auth: email and/or Apple ID)
  • PSA values, dates, notes you enter
  • Standard Firebase service logs

We do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • Show and sync your readings
  • Run and secure the app
  • Answer support emails

Where it’s stored

  • On your device (local cache)
  • In Google Cloud Firestore when signed in (linked to your account ID)

Third parties

  • Google Firebase (auth + database)
  • Sign in with Apple (if you use it)

Deletion

  • Delete one reading in the app
  • Settings → Clear all data (device + cloud)
  • Email YOUR_EMAIL@example.com to request deletion

Security: HTTPS and Firebase rules; only your account can access your readings.

Children: Not for under 13.

Contact: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com
